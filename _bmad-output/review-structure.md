## Reduction summary

Eight recommendations. If all are accepted, the estimated reduction is **about 320 words**, approximately **11.1%** of the exact 2,870-word total. This is an estimate because only the total and §3, §4.4 FR-8, and §7 counts were supplied as exact section metrics; per-finding savings are estimates and rounded. No length target was provided. The proposed cuts preserve reader scaffolding, the evaluation coverage caveat, and human-approval distinctions; the only reduced engagement is removal of meta-explanation in §2.3, replaced by a direct statement of the core journey.

## Author triage

All recommendations were applied: the PRD now leads with an at-a-glance summary, keeps one concise reviewer journey, retains a compact glossary, separates evaluation method from the scorecard, combines scope boundaries, and condenses guardrails and assumptions without removing the coverage caveats.
