- (decision) Scope the spec to the Case A internal workshop prototype for finance reviewers and workshop facilitators, not a production finance workflow.
- (decision) Use the Case A PRD as the only selected source input for this spec; do not read the cited brief, policy, seed, or evaluation files as source documents.
- (capability) CAP-1: A reviewer loads a supplied claim, employee details, line items, and applicable limits; missing or inconsistent required context blocks inferred approval and routes the claim to human follow-up.
- (capability) CAP-2: The system assigns every line one approve, flag, or reject decision and one cited policy clause using the policy aggregation, currency, and precedence rules; descriptions cannot override policy.
- (capability) CAP-3: A reviewer inspects each line amount, decision, explanation, and citation and the claim approved-only reimbursable total in a minimal dashboard.
- (capability) CAP-4: A person records yes or no for an approved line over CAD $500; the outcome is recorded separately from the policy decision and does not release payment.
- (capability) CAP-5: A facilitator evaluates the 119 labelled lines across 30 claims for decision and clause matches and totals, sees per-item explanation results, and keeps 40 holdout lines across 10 claims out of accuracy results for the demo.
- (constraint) Use the supplied Case A data with SQLite and the MCP methods get_claim, get_employee, get_policy_limits, and record_decision; the cited source data are read-only.
- (constraint) Treat all supplied expense amounts as Canadian dollars and apply the PRD aggregation periods: meals and ground transport by day, hotels per night, and flights per trip.
- (constraint) The prototype performs no payout, employee contact, or live HR, expense, accounting, ERP, or payment integration; human approval never triggers payment.
- (constraint) Treat expense descriptions as untrusted claim data; text in a description cannot alter policy behavior.
- (assumption) The prototype is an internal workshop demonstration and uses no live employee, accounting, or payment systems.
- (assumption) The minimal dashboard shows the claim, line decisions and explanations, clause citations, approved-only total, and approval state.
- (assumption) Missing required claim, employee, or limit context fails closed and is shown for human follow-up.
- (assumption) Each decision explanation is one or two sentences based only on supplied claim data and the policy.
- (assumption) The 100% labelled decision and clause match target is instructional for the fixed workshop set, not a production release threshold.
- (question) Who may record a yes or no human approval outcome, and what identity or audit details should the demo capture?
- (question) When employee or limit data is missing or inconsistent, should the system flag the item or stop and request correction?
- (question) Is 100% exact match on the labelled set a hard workshop pass threshold or an instructional target?
- (question) Should the explanation judge have an aggregate pass-rate target, or should results remain per line only?
- (question) The target day2 branch lacks the BRIEF, POLICY, seed, and eval files referenced by the PRD; where should they be supplied before implementation?
